FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about LibraSmart.

How do I borrow a book?▼
Register or log in, browse the catalog, click on any available book, and hit "Borrow This Book". You'll get a confirmation with your due date instantly.
How many books can I borrow at once?▼
By default, members can borrow up to 5 books simultaneously. Your institution's librarian can adjust this limit based on your membership tier.
What happens if I return a book late?▼
A fine of PKR 2 per day per book is charged for late returns. Fines must be cleared before borrowing new books. You can renew online before your due date to avoid fines.
Can I renew my borrowed book online?▼
Yes! Go to "My Books", find the book, and click "Renew". Each book can be renewed once, adding 14 more days to your loan period — as long as no one has reserved it.
How do I set up LibraSmart for my institution?▼
Contact us via the form above or email support@librasmart.com. We'll help you configure the system, import your existing catalog, and set up admin accounts for your team.
Is my data secure?▼
Absolutely. All data is stored securely in a SQL Server database with encrypted connections. Passwords are hashed and sessions are protected. We never share your data with third parties.
